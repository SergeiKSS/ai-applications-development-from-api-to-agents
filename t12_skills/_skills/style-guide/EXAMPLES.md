# Style Guide Examples

## Email Subject Lines

| Before (❌)                                          | After (✅)                     | Rule Applied                          |
|-------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| We are pleased to announce our new feature release!!! | New: faster exports are here    | Cut hype/jargon, drop excess punctuation |
| Important Notice Regarding Your Account Status        | Your account needs a quick update | Plain English, shorter, direct        |
| Don't Miss Out On This Amazing Opportunity             | Save 20% through Friday         | Drop clickbait, lead with the benefit  |
| Reminder: Action May Be Required On Your Part          | Please confirm your email       | Active voice, clear single action      |

## Email Body Rewrites

**Before (❌):**
> We are pleased to inform you that your request has been processed successfully by our dedicated
> support team. Should you require any further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.

**After (✅):**
> Good news — we've processed your request. Need anything else? Just reply to this email and we'll
> help right away.

**Changes:** Removed passive voice ("has been processed by") and stiff phrasing ("please do not
hesitate"), switched to "we/you", shortened from 2 long sentences to 2 short ones.

## Blog Heading Rewrites

| Before (❌, label-style)      | After (✅, outcome-focused)         |
|--------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Deployment Optimization         | Cut deploy time in half               |
| Introduction to Caching         | Make your app feel instant with caching |
| Error Handling Best Practices   | Stop silent failures from reaching users |
| API Rate Limiting Overview      | Keep your API fast under heavy traffic |

## Common Jargon → Plain English

| Jargon                        | Plain English            |
|---------------------------------|---------------------------|
| Leverage synergies              | Work together             |
| Utilize                         | Use                       |
| At this point in time            | Now                        |
| Facilitate                      | Help                      |
| In order to                     | To                        |
| Circle back                     | Follow up                 |
| Going forward                   | From now on                |
| Pursuant to your request         | As you asked               |

## Social Media Rewrites

**LinkedIn — Before (❌):**
> We are excited to announce the launch of our new product update which includes several
> improvements to performance and user experience. #innovation #technology #business

**LinkedIn — After (✅):**
> Your dashboard just got 3x faster. We rebuilt the loading pipeline from scratch — here's what
> changed and why it matters for your daily workflow.
> #ProductUpdate #Performance #Engineering

**Changes:** Opened with a concrete hook instead of "we are excited to announce", cut vague
buzzwords ("innovation", "business"), moved hashtags to the end and limited to 3 relevant ones.

**Twitter/X — Before (❌):**
> Hello everyone! We wanted to take a moment to let you know that we have just released a brand
> new feature that we think you are going to love. Check it out!

**Twitter/X — After (✅):**
> New: dark mode is live. Toggle it in Settings → Appearance.

**Changes:** Dropped the greeting and lead-in, led with the point, cut to one idea under 280
characters.

## Support Reply Rewrites

**Before (❌):**
> It appears the setting was misconfigured on your end, which caused the error. You will need to
> navigate to the settings page and correct the configuration.

**After (✅):**
> Thanks for flagging this — that error is frustrating, and we see why. Let's fix it together:
> 1. Open **Settings → Billing**.
> 2. Toggle **Auto-renew** back on.
> 3. Refresh the page — the error should be gone.
>
> Still stuck? Reply here and we'll jump in.

**Changes:** Replaced blame-shifting passive phrasing with empathy-first language ("Thanks for
flagging this"), turned the fix into numbered steps with bolded UI labels, added a closing offer
for further help.
